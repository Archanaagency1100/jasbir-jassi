Jasbir Jassi · Gurdaspur, Punjab

The artist

The person
behind the voice.

Born Jasbir Singh Bains in Gurdaspur, Punjab, Jasbir Jassi is a singer, lyricist, performer and actor whose work spans Punjabi folk, Sufi music and cinema.

His recording journey began with Channa Ve Teri Channani in 1993. Dil Le Gayee followed in 1998, bringing his voice to a wider audience.
